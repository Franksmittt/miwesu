================================================================================
MIWESU — BRAND LOOK & FEEL (for ads, social, and marketing)
================================================================================
Use this when creating Facebook ads, Instagram posts, or any marketing so everything feels like the same brand as the website.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. COLOUR PALETTE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Use these exact colours so ads match the site.

  PRIMARY (use for accents, CTAs, key words, borders, icons)
  - Rich Gold:     #C5A059  (main gold — buttons, highlights, "gradient" words)
  - Pale Gold:     #E5C687  (lighter accent, subtext)
  - Dark Antique:  #997B3D  (darker gold for depth)

  BACKGROUNDS
  - Onyx (black):  #050505  (heroes, dark sections, premium feel)
  - Onyx light:    #121212  (cards, panels on dark)
  - Marble:        #FAFAFA  (light sections, body background)

  TEXT
  - Headlines on dark:  white #FFFFFF
  - Body on dark:       grey  #D1D5DB or #9CA3AF
  - Body on light:      near-black #111111

  RULE: Dark backgrounds + gold accents + white/grey text = premium. Avoid bright or neon colours.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. TYPOGRAPHY STYLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  HEADLINES
  - Serif, elegant, editorial (e.g. Cinzel or similar).
  - One or two words can be in gold or gold-gradient for emphasis (e.g. "Iron Eden", "Heat. Redefined.").
  - Often large and sparse — don’t crowd the headline.

  BODY / SUPPORTING TEXT
  - Clean sans-serif (e.g. Montserrat or similar).
  - Light or regular weight for a refined feel; avoid heavy black type.

  LABELS / EYEBROWS (small text above headlines)
  - UPPERCASE.
  - Wide letter-spacing (e.g. 0.3em–0.5em).
  - Gold or pale gold colour.
  - Small font size (e.g. 10px–12px).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. TONE OF VOICE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Premium but not stuffy: "sanctuary", "peerless", "crafted", "redefined".
  - Bushveld / African: "Makoppa", "sweetveld", "koppies", "Iron Eden".
  - Short, confident lines: "Heat. Redefined." / "A peerless sanctuary of silence."
  - Avoid: slang, hypey sales speak, all caps for whole sentences.
  - Do: one clear idea per line, room to breathe, gold on one or two key words.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. IMAGERY STYLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Real photography: landscape, wildlife, accommodation, firewood, people on the farm.
  - Mood: warm, natural, golden hour where possible; not cold or overly filtered.
  - On dark backgrounds: images often slightly darkened (e.g. 40–50% opacity or gradient overlay) so white/gold text reads clearly.
  - Frames: thin white or gold borders (e.g. border-white/5 or gold at low opacity) can echo the site’s "frame" look.
  - Avoid: clip art, loud graphics, cluttered layouts. Keep one strong focal point per ad.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. LAYOUT & COMPOSITION (for ad creative)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Plenty of negative space; don’t fill every pixel.
  - Headline + one short line of support + CTA is enough.
  - Align to centre for hero-style ads; left-align for carousel or text-heavy units.
  - Gold line or thin divider under headlines (like the site) works well.
  - Buttons: gold background (#C5A059 or gradient to #997B3D), white text, rounded corners.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. FIREWOOD / THERMAL SITE (standalone-thermal)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  That part of the brand uses a similar dark + copper/gold feel:
  - Copper: #B87333 (primary), deeper #8E4000.
  - Same idea: dark background, warm metal accent, clean type.
  - Copy: "Heat. Redefined.", "Certified dry.", "Free delivery in Gauteng."
  - Use for any firewood/braai ads so they feel consistent with the thermal site.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. QUICK CHECKLIST FOR EACH AD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  [ ] Background dark (onyx) or light (marble), not random bright colours.
  [ ] Gold/copper used for accent only (CTA, one or two words, or thin lines).
  [ ] Headline serif or strong sans; one key word in gold if it fits.
  [ ] Uppercase, spaced-out label above headline if you have room.
  [ ] Real photography, slightly muted on dark for readability.
  [ ] Short, confident copy; no long paragraphs.
  [ ] CTA button: gold, white text, simple wording ("Inquire", "Order Now", "Get Allocation").

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
