FAQ items (full text also in JSON-LD — lib/home-faq-data.ts)
1. What is the ethical caliber for Cape Buffalo in Thabazimbi?
   → SA minimum for dangerous game (.375 H&H class); PH/regulations; concierge;
     not legal/PH advice.
2. Do you handle SAPS 520 firearm permits?
   → Free PDF tool on site; submission/approval is with SAPS; MIWESU does not
     issue permits.
3. Is MIWESU malaria-free?
   → Waterberg widely low/none vs many lowveld areas; confirm with travel clinic.
4. How do I book an exclusive-use stay?
   → Enquiry-first, no public checkout; book or contact flow.
5. What plains game species are available?
   → 14+ species, rifle/bow, quota, Guardian’s Pledge; wildlife hub / compare.
