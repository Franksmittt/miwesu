All species offered for hunting at the lodge/farm. Use for ads, brochures, and

SUMMARY FOR ADS / ONE-LINERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • 14 species: Greater Kudu, Blue Wildebeest, Golden Wildebeest, Impala,
    Dapple Impala, Gemsbok, Warthog, Blesbok, Bushbuck, Cape Buffalo, Lechwe,
    Livingstone Eland, Red Hartebeest, Springbok.
  • Big Five representation: Cape Buffalo (dangerous game).
  • Plains game: Wildebeest, Impala, Gemsbok, Blesbok, Springbok, Red Hartebeest.
  • Thicket / bush: Kudu, Bushbuck, Warthog.
  • Variants: Dapple Impala, Golden Wildebeest.
  • “We do not sell animals; we manage an ecosystem. Availability is dictated
    by our annual ecological census.”

PRICING (FROM SITE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Greater Kudu: R 12,000 – R 25,000 (horn length).
  • Blue Wildebeest: from R 5,500 per trophy.
  • Impala: from R 2,500 per trophy.
  • Gemsbok, Warthog, Eland, Red Hartebeest: detailed fees in the confidential
    Conservation Investment Guide (on request / vetted guests).
  • Trophy fees include: professional tracking, field preparation, meat processing.
  • 100% of meat from harvests is donated to local communities.
