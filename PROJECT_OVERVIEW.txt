1. WHAT THIS PROJECT IS
================================================================================

A production-oriented marketing + operations web application for MIWESU GAME
FARM ("Iron Eden"): a private luxury sanctuary in the Makoppa district near
Thabazimbi, Limpopo, South Africa (Waterberg, malaria-free bushveld).

Public site:
  - Two exclusive-use residences (Homestead ~16 sleepers, Rooibok Kraal / Stone
    Villa ~6 sleepers), activities, conservation story, 14 species guides,
    gallery, blog ("Hunter's Journal"), tools (SAPS 520, biltong calculator),
    wood products, partners, trophy export guidance.
  - Booking is enquiry-first (no public checkout for lodge stays); public rates
    are presented as "on request" where configured.

Admin / backend:
  - Password-gated admin: bookings CRM, calendar, rates manager, pricelist PDF,
    email diagnostics. Prisma + PostgreSQL (e.g. Supabase). Resend for outbound
    mail. Invoice PDF and SAPS 520 PDF generation. Stripe present in codebase
    but not the primary lodge enquiry flow.

Locale:
  - Primary English site; dedicated landing routes /de and /es for international
    reach with hreflang from root metadata.

2. TECH STACK (AS OF NOW)
================================================================================

Core:

4. WHERE THE PRODUCT STANDS (MATURITY)
================================================================================

Strengths:
  - Broad marketing surface: home, about, residences (+ sub-pages), wildlife
    hub, 14 dedicated species URLs, compare tool, conservation, gallery, blog
    (dynamic slugs + two static pillar articles), FAQ, contact, book flow,
    availability, rates (on-request presentation), wood, trophy-export,
    partners, tools subtree.
  - Admin workflow for owners: bookings list/detail, status workflow, calendar,
    manual booking creation, rates editing, pricelist export, email logging.
  - SEO investment: metadata per segment, canonicals, Open Graph / Twitter
    images centralised (lib/open-graph.ts), sitemap covering static routes,
    blog slugs, species; robots.txt blocks admin/API appropriately in production.
  - Structured data: Organization, WebSite, LodgingBusiness (home), species
    WebPage entities, FAQPage, Article on blog routes, Product samples on rates,
    TouristTrip on activities, wood Product graph, gallery ItemList, WebPage
    JSON-LD on many marketing layouts, dynamic compare WebPage + metadata for
    ?a=&b= pairs.
  - Google Search Console verification supported via
    NEXT_PUBLIC_GOOGLE_SITE_VERIFICATION when set.

Operational notes:
  - Requires valid DATABASE_URL (and Resend keys) for full admin/email flows;
    empty DB may fall back to mock patterns in places (see code/docs).
  - Image assets: many paths are curated under public/images; blog/species use
    real card and lodge photography where mapped.
