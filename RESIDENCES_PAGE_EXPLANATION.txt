RESIDENCES PAGE — COMPLETE EXPLANATION
=======================================
URL: http://localhost:3000/residences (or /residences on the live site)

This document describes the Residences page in full: what it looks like, what it contains, how it is structured, and what each part is for. It is written so that someone who cannot see the screen can understand the whole page.

---
1. PURPOSE OF THE PAGE
---

The Residences page is the main accommodation hub for MIWESU Game Farm. It presents the two private residences (The Homestead and The Stone Villa), their facilities, amenities, and how to book or inquire. The page aims to convey exclusive use, bespoke living, daily housekeeping, and absolute privacy.

---
2. OVERALL LAYOUT AND WRAPPER
---

The page sits inside the site-wide Layout. That means:
- At the top: the main site navigation (logo, menu: Stay, Experience, Practical, Journal, Reach, etc.).
- The main content fills the centre of the screen.
- At the bottom: the site footer (brand, tagline, infinite scroll of images, links to Stay, Experience, Concierge, Reach us, contact, social).

The page content itself has a light marble/off-white background for the outer wrapper, with a large dark (onyx) section in the middle that holds most of the content.

11. COMPONENTS AND FEATURES (TECHNICAL)
---

- Layout: The page uses the global Layout component (navigation + main + footer).
- Images: Next.js Image component; the hero and facility images use priority or lazy loading as appropriate. Facility images have a fallback if the primary image fails to load (FacilityImage helper).
- Reveal on scroll: Sections use a "reveal" class so that as you scroll, they animate into view (simple scroll-triggered visibility).
- Links: All "View The Homestead", "View The Stone Villa", "Book your stay", "Inquire", "Request Availability", and "View Rates" are proper links to the routes listed above.
- Responsive behaviour: Grids collapse to one or two columns on small screens; the two main residence cards stack vertically on mobile; the housekeeping and location blocks stack on small screens.
- Styling: Dark (onyx) background for the main content area, gold for accents and labels, white/gray for text; serif (Cinzel) for headings, sans (Montserrat) for body; borders and hover states (e.g. card border turns gold on hover, images scale on hover).
