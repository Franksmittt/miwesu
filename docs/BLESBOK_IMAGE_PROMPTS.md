# Blesbok (Damaliscus pygargus phillipsi) – 6 image prompts

Use these prompts to generate images. Save as PNG in `public/images/` with the exact filenames below. You can add a number prefix (e.g. `1blesbok-ram-portrait-01.png`) if you like; the page can be updated to match.

## 1. `blesbok-ram-portrait-01.png`
**Hero / dramatic portrait**
- Close-up portrait of a mature **Blesbok ram** (Damaliscus pygargus phillipsi) in natural light.
- Face and shoulders filling the frame; rich brown coat, **white blaze** on face (distinctive blesbok marking from forehead to nose), lyre-shaped horns clearly visible, reddish-brown on shoulders and flanks.
- Open grassland or Highveld background, shallow depth of field. Professional wildlife photography style, sharp focus on eyes and horns. No people, no fences.

## 2. `blesbok-ram-standing-01.png`
**Ram in habitat**

- Full body or three-quarter view; white face blaze, lyre-shaped horns, brown coat with darker patches on shoulders and flanks, pale belly.
- Golden grass, open plains or savanna. Professional wildlife photo, natural colours. No people, no vehicles.
