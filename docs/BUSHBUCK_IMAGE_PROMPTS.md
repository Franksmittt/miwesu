# Bushbuck (Tragelaphus sylvaticus) – 6 image prompts

Use these prompts to generate images. Save as PNG in `public/images/` with the exact filenames below. You can add a number prefix (e.g. `1bushbuck-ram-portrait-01.png`); the page can be updated to match.

---

## 1. `bushbuck-ram-portrait-01.png`
**Hero / dramatic portrait**
- Close-up portrait of a mature **Bushbuck ram** (Tragelaphus sylvaticus) in natural light.
- Face and shoulders filling the frame; reddish-brown to dark coat with **white spots and stripes** (especially on flanks and legs), **spiral horns** (males only), shy, compact build.
- Riverine thicket, forest edge, or dense bush background, shallow depth of field. Professional wildlife photography style, sharp focus on eyes and horns. No people, no fences.

## 2. `bushbuck-ram-standing-01.png`
**Ram in habitat**
- Single **Bushbuck ram** standing or moving in **riverine thicket, forest edge, or dense bush**.
- Full body or three-quarter view; spiral horns, spotted/striped coat, compact body, often in dappled shade.
- Thicket, reeds, or woodland. Professional wildlife photo, natural colours. No people, no vehicles.

## 3. `bushbuck-ewe-bushveld-01.png`

- Grazing or standing alert; may have lamb nearby or not. Dense vegetation, natural light. Professional wildlife style. No people.
