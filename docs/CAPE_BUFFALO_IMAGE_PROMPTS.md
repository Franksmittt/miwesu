# Cape Buffalo (Syncerus caffer) – 6 image prompts

Use these prompts to generate images. Save as PNG in `public/images/` with the exact filenames below. You can add a number prefix (e.g. `1cape-buffalo-bull-portrait-01.png`) if you like; the page can be updated to match.

---

## 1. `cape-buffalo-bull-portrait-01.png`
**Hero / dramatic portrait**
- Close-up portrait of a mature **Cape Buffalo bull** (Syncerus caffer) in natural light.
- Face and boss (horn base) filling the frame; dark grey-black coat, massive curved horns with fused boss, ears and eyes clear. Bushveld or grassland background, shallow depth of field.
- Professional wildlife photography style, sharp focus on eyes and horns. Conveys power and presence. No people, no fences.

---

## 2. `cape-buffalo-bull-standing-01.png`
**Bull in habitat**
- Single **Cape Buffalo bull** standing or walking in African bushveld or grassland.
- Full body or three-quarter view; heavy build, broad boss and sweeping horns visible; dark coat, thick neck.
- Golden grass, scrub or open savanna. Professional wildlife photo, natural colours. No people, no vehicles.

## 3. `cape-buffalo-cow-bushveld-01.png`
**Female in habitat**
- **Cape Buffalo cow** (female) in bushveld or grassland. Slightly smaller than bull, horns less massive and no fully fused boss.
- Grazing or standing alert; may have calf nearby or not. Bushveld vegetation, natural light.
- Professional wildlife style. No people.

- Bushveld or grass backdrop. Professional wildlife portrait. No people.
