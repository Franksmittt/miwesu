**Hero / dramatic portrait**
- Close-up portrait of a mature **Golden Wildebeest bull** (colour variant of Connochaetes taurinus) in natural light.
- Face and shoulders filling the frame; **golden, copper, or honey-coloured coat** (not grey/brindled—distinct from Blue Wildebeest), matching golden mane, beard, and tail; robust horns (boss), heavy build.
