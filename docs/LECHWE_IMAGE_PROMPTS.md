# Lechwe (Kobus leche) – 6 image prompts

Use these prompts to generate images. Save as PNG in `public/images/` with the exact filenames below. You can add a number prefix (e.g. `1lechwe-ram-portrait-01.png`); the page can be updated to match.

## 1. `lechwe-ram-portrait-01.png`
**Hero / dramatic portrait**
- Close-up portrait of a mature **Lechwe ram** (Kobus leche) in natural light.
- Face and shoulders filling the frame; reddish-brown coat, **long lyre-shaped horns** (characteristic of lechwe), elongated face, often in or near wetland habitat.
- Wetland edge, floodplain, or grassland background, shallow depth of field. Professional wildlife photography style, sharp focus on eyes and horns. No people, no fences.

- Reeds, water, or damp grassland. Professional wildlife photo, natural colours. No people, no vehicles.
