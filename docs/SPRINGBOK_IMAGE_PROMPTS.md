# Springbok (Antidorcas marsupialis) – 6 image prompts

Use these prompts to generate images. Save as PNG in `public/images/` with the exact filenames below. You can add a number prefix (e.g. `1springbok-ram-portrait-01.png`) if you like; the page can be updated to match.

---

## 1. `springbok-ram-portrait-01.png`
**Hero / dramatic portrait**
- Close-up portrait of a mature **Springbok ram** (Antidorcas marsupialis) in natural light.
- Face and shoulders filling the frame; pale brown and white coat, white face with dark stripe from eye to mouth, lyre-shaped horns clearly visible, dark patch on the flank.
- Open plains or grassland background, shallow depth of field. Professional wildlife photography style, sharp focus on eyes and horns. No people, no fences.

---

## 2. `springbok-ram-standing-01.png`
**Ram in habitat**
- Single **Springbok ram** standing or walking in African grassland or open plains.
- Full body or three-quarter view; lyre-shaped horns, white face markings, dark flank stripe and dorsal (back) stripe visible.
- Golden grass, arid savanna or Karoo-style vegetation. Professional wildlife photo, natural colours. No people, no vehicles.

---

## 3. `springbok-ewe-bushveld-01.png`
**Female in habitat**
- **Springbok ewe** (female) in grassland or open bushveld. Smaller, slighter build than ram; no horns or very small horns; same white face and flank markings.
- Grazing or standing alert; may have lamb nearby or not. Plains or grassland, natural light.
- Professional wildlife style. No people.

---

## 4. `springbok-ram-portrait-02.png`
**Second portrait / horn and markings**
- Close-up of a **Springbok ram** with emphasis on **horn shape** (lyre) and facial/body markings.
- Horns clearly visible: black, lyre-shaped, ridged; white face, dark eye stripe; pale back with dark dorsal stripe; fold (pouch) along back optional.
- Grassland or plains backdrop. Professional wildlife portrait. No people.

---

## 5. `springbok-herd-bushveld-01.png`
**Group / behaviour**
- **Springbok herd** (several rams and ewes) in grassland or open plains.
- Grazing, walking, or one animal pronking (leaping with arched back, four legs extended) if possible. Wide shot showing habitat and herd.
- Professional wildlife photography. No people, no infrastructure.

---

## 6. `springbok-ram-standing-02.png`
**Ram at water / grassland**
- **Springbok ram** standing or walking near a **waterhole, pan, or in open grassland** (African plains or bushveld).
- Full or three-quarter body; horns and white markings clear. Early morning or late afternoon light.
- Professional wildlife photo. No people, no infrastructure.
