# Warthog (Phacochoerus africanus) – 6 image prompts

Use these prompts to generate images. Save as PNG in `public/images/` with the exact filenames below. You can add a number prefix (e.g. `1warthog-boar-portrait-01.png`) if you like; the page can be updated to match.

---

## 1. `warthog-boar-portrait-01.png`
**Hero / dramatic portrait**
- Close-up portrait of a mature **Warthog boar** (Phacochoerus africanus) in natural light.
- Face and shoulders filling the frame; grey bristly coat, prominent facial warts, upward-curving tusks clearly visible, mane along the spine.
- Bushveld or grassland background, shallow depth of field. Professional wildlife photography style, sharp focus on eyes and tusks. No people, no fences.

## 2. `warthog-boar-standing-01.png`
**Boar in habitat**
- Single **Warthog boar** standing or walking in African bushveld or grassland.
- Full body or three-quarter view; tail raised (characteristic “flag” pose optional); tusks visible; warts and mane clear.
- Golden grass, scrub or open savanna. Professional wildlife photo, natural colours. No people, no vehicles.

## 3. `warthog-sow-bushveld-01.png`
**Female in habitat**

- Grazing or standing alert; may have young (piglets) nearby or not. Bushveld vegetation, natural light.
- Professional wildlife style. No people.

## 4. `warthog-boar-portrait-02.png`
**Second portrait / tusk detail**

- Tusks clearly visible: upper tusks curving up and out, lower tusks sharp (used for digging and defence); facial warts, bristles, mane.
- Bushveld or grass backdrop. Professional wildlife portrait. No people.

## 5. `warthog-family-bushveld-01.png`
**Group / behaviour**

- Piglets may be striped; adults grey. Grazing, trotting, or standing; tail-up pose if possible.
- Wide shot showing habitat and behaviour. Professional wildlife photography. No people, no fences.
