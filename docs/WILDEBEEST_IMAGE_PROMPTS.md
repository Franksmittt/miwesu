# Blue Wildebeest (Connochaetes taurinus) – 6 image prompts

Use these prompts to generate images. Save as PNG in `public/images/` with the exact filenames below.

---

## 1. `wildebeest-bull-portrait-01.png`
**Hero / dramatic portrait**
- Close-up portrait of a mature **Blue Wildebeest bull** (Connochaetes taurinus) in natural light.
- Face and shoulders filling the frame; bluish-grey coat, black mane, heavy curved horns, beard under chin.
- Soft bushveld or grassland background, shallow depth of field. Professional wildlife photography style, sharp focus on eyes and horns. No people, no fences.

---

## 2. `wildebeest-bull-standing-01.png`
**Bull on open plains**
- Single **Blue Wildebeest bull** standing on open African grassland/savanna.
- Full body or three-quarter view; standing alert, horns visible (broad at base, curving out then up).
- Golden grass, clear sky or soft clouds. Sense of space and plains. Professional wildlife photo, natural colours. No people, no vehicles.

---

## 3. `wildebeest-cow-bushveld-01.png`
**Female in habitat**
- **Blue Wildebeest cow** (female) in bushveld or grassland habitat.
- Lighter build than bull, chestnut-coloured forehead (not jet black); horns similar shape but more slender.
- Surrounded by grass and maybe light scrub. Peaceful, natural grazing or standing. Professional wildlife style. No people.

---

## 4. `wildebeest-bull-portrait-02.png`
**Second portrait / horn detail**
- Another close-up of a **Blue Wildebeest bull**, emphasis on **horn configuration** and head structure.
- Horns clearly visible: broad base, ridged (annulated), curved outward then upward then inward (handlebar shape).
- Bluish-grey coat, black mane, beard. Bushveld or grass backdrop. Professional wildlife portrait. No people.

---

## 5. `wildebeest-herd-bushveld-01.png`
**Herd / behaviour**
- Small **group of Blue Wildebeest** (5–10 animals) in grassland or open bushveld.
- Mix of bulls and cows if possible; some grazing, some standing. Dust or grass in the air optional.
- Wide shot showing habitat and social behaviour. Professional wildlife photography. No people, no fences.

---

## 6. `wildebeest-bull-standing-02.png`
**Bull in grassland / grazing**
- **Blue Wildebeest bull** standing or walking in **Sweetveld-style grassland** (golden or green grass, African plains).
- Full body or three-quarter view; horns and distinctive silhouette clear.
- Early morning or late afternoon light. Professional wildlife photo. No people, no infrastructure.
